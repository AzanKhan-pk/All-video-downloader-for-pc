Frequently asked questions

VidLoom FAQ

Answers to common questions about VidLoom, public media links, formats, failed downloads and responsible use.

Is VidLoom free?

The current public tool is available without a subscription. Hosting and available features can change as the project develops.

Why does TikTok or another platform sometimes fail?

Platforms regularly change page structures and access rules. A public link can also be region-limited, removed, login-restricted or temporarily incompatible with the extractor used by the server.

Does every link work?

No. Compatibility depends on the source, whether the media is public, and whether a usable format can be extracted.

Why is a requested quality unavailable?

VidLoom cannot create a real higher-quality source if the platform did not make that quality available. The best compatible source may be selected instead.

Can private or protected content be downloaded?

No. VidLoom is intended for public media and should not be used to bypass access controls.
