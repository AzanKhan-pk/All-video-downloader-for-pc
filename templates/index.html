06 / Download guide

How to use the
video downloader.

All Video Downloader is designed for public media that you are allowed to download. Always respect copyright, platform rules and the rights of creators.

01

Copy a public link

Open a supported public video or audio page and copy its complete URL from the address bar or share menu.

02

Paste and inspect

Paste the URL into All Video Downloader and select Fetch media. The site checks the public link and displays available information.

03

Choose a format

Select video or audio and choose an available quality. Higher resolutions depend on the source and server processing tools.

07 / Frequently asked questions

Useful answers,
before you download.

Is All Video Downloader free?
All Video Downloader provides its public-media tool without a subscription on the current website.

Why does a link sometimes fail?
A platform may change its public page, require login, restrict automated access, or remove the media. Available formats can also differ by video.

Can every quality be downloaded?
No. The source decides which qualities and formats are available. All Video Downloader can only process compatible formats that are actually available from the public source.

Can private or login-only media be downloaded?
No. The site is intended for public media and does not provide a way to bypass private access or account restrictions.
